Understanding the Four Seasons

An overview of how nature changes beautifully throughout the year.

Spring

Spring is the season of renewal. Flowers bloom, trees turn green, and the air feels fresh and full of life.

Summer

Summer brings warmth and long sunny days. It's the perfect time for vacations, outdoor fun, and enjoying nature’s brightness.

Autumn

Autumn, also called fall, is known for its colorful leaves and cool winds. It's a season of harvest and transition.

Winter

Winter is the coldest season of the year, often bringing snow, cozy nights, and festive celebrations.

Fun Facts

  • Each season lasts about three months.

Why Seasons Matter

Seasons affect our climate, agriculture, lifestyle, and festivals. Each one brings its own charm and significance to life on Earth.
